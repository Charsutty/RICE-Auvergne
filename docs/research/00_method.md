# Historical research method

## Purpose

Research for RICE: Auvergne must separate historical evidence from gameplay interpretation.

Each research note should use the following categories:

### A — Documented
A claim directly supported by a primary source, archaeological publication, scholarly article, institutional catalogue, or reliable prosopographical database.

### B — Plausible reconstruction
A synthesis inferred from documented structures, chronology, geography, or relationships, but not itself directly attested.

### C — Gameplay fiction
A fictionalized situation designed to represent a documented social or political pattern without claiming that the particular event occurred.

## Entry format

For each usable fact record:

- **Claim**
- **Date / period**
- **Place / persons**
- **Evidence class**: A / B / C
- **Source**
- **Gameplay relevance**
- **Open questions**

## Research priorities

1. Verify CK3 title and province mapping before attaching local content to a title.
2. Prefer scholarly and primary-source editions over unsourced genealogy websites.
3. Treat medieval family reconstructions cautiously where filiation is debated.
4. Do not make Saugues or Mercœur artificially central: their content should reflect their actual role in the highland frontier and Auvergnat/Gévaudanais networks.
5. Maintain chronology: content appropriate to 867 should not assume institutions that only emerged in the twelfth or thirteenth century.
6. Record uncertainty explicitly.
